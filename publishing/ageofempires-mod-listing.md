## Description

<h2>Crown of Ash</h2>

<p>A fully AI-scripted random map for Age of Empires II: Definitive Edition,
designed by betwixtX with OpenAI Codex. Created as a public proof that modern AI
can design, script, debug, validate, and ship a complete AoE2 DE map.</p>

<p>Every player starts in a balanced clearing inside dense pine forest. Roads
lead toward a raised central Crown where rich mines, five relics, and hostile
neutral wardens create an early strategic objective.</p>

<p>Capture the indestructible Crown Monument to earn 24 gold per minute. Explore
and cut through the forest to discover four guarded ash caches containing
randomized bonus resources. Team games also receive narrow rear roads between
allied clearings.</p>

<h3>Recommended settings</h3>

<ul>
  <li>2-8 players</li>
  <li>Random Map or Regicide</li>
  <li>Standard resources</li>
  <li>Normal recommended map size for the player count</li>
  <li>AI opponents supported</li>
</ul>

<p>This map targets Definitive Edition Update 153015 or newer and is entirely
self-contained for multiplayer lobby transfer.</p>
