CROWN OF ASH v1.1.0-rc1 SMOKE TEST
==================================

PURPOSE
-------

This candidate restores every original Crown of Ash feature after correcting
the confirmed crash cause: base_elevation was used without the required
<ELEVATION_GENERATION> section.

INSTALL
-------

Copy Crown-of-Ash-v1.1.0-rc1.rms into:

%USERPROFILE%\Games\Age of Empires 2 DE\<player-id>\resources\_common\random-map-scripts\

Remove or rename earlier Crown of Ash test files if the map list is ambiguous,
then fully restart Age of Empires II: Definitive Edition. No PNG is required.

TEST A: CRASH REGRESSION
------------------------

Start a single-player Skirmish with the same AI, map size, game mode, and
Reveal Map: All Visible settings used for E01 and E02.

- Scroll across the entire map.
- Select and move the scout and villagers.
- Move the scout to the raised center and each visible cache.
- Let the match run for at least five minutes.

Result:  SKIRMISH _____  TIME __________

TEST B: COMPLETE GAMEPLAY
-------------------------

Start a new Random Map Skirmish with normal fog of war and at least one AI.

- Confirm the Town Center, villagers, scout, herdables, forage, boar, deer,
  gold, stone, and pine trees appear in each explored starting clearing.
- Follow the guaranteed road to the raised central Crown.
- Confirm hostile wolves, jaguars, or dire wolves guard the center.
- Fight the wardens and capture the indestructible Monument.
- Stop every other source of gold income, record the current amount, hold the
  Monument for one minute, and confirm a 24-gold gain.
- Confirm the central gold and stone piles contain 1,000 and 500 resources.
- Find at least one hidden ash cache and confirm its mixed treasure and guard.
- Continue playing, building, fighting, and exploring for at least ten minutes.

Result:  SKIRMISH _____  TIME __________

EDITOR LIMITATIONS
------------------

The Scenario Editor test mode does not apply set_gaia_unconvertible and does
not show resource_delta changes. Use Skirmish for the warden hostility and
enriched-mine checks.
