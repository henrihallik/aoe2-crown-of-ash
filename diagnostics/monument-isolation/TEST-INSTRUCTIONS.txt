CROWN OF ASH MONUMENT ISOLATION
================================

OBSOLETE: DO NOT USE THESE RESULTS TO BLAME THE MONUMENT.

After T01 and M01 failed, the unchanged v1.0.2 baseline was also confirmed to
crash when a scout approached the center. Both tests inherited that fault, so
neither result isolates its added building. Use the newer terrain-isolation
tests first.

INSTALL
-------

Copy the individual M-test .rms files into:

%USERPROFILE%\Games\Age of Empires 2 DE\<player-id>\resources\_common\random-map-scripts\

Fully restart Age of Empires II: Definitive Edition. No PNG files are needed.

TEST ORDER
----------

Use Scenario Editor, generate the named map, press Test, move a unit, and wait
at least two minutes before recording PASS.

M01 Outpost Same Placement
    Ordinary building control with every placement attribute used by failed T01.
    PASS means the placement recipe itself is valid.

M02 Bare Symbolic Monument
    Uses only: create_object MONUMENT
    CRASH after an M01 PASS identifies the Monument object itself.

M03 Monument Crown Land
    Adds place_on_specific_land_id.

M04 Monument Crown Land Gaia
    Adds set_gaia_object_only.

M05 Monument Crown Land Gaia Ignore
    Adds ignore_terrain_restrictions.

M06 Monument Full Placement
    Adds find_closest_to_map_center and is symbolically equivalent to T01.

M07 Capturable Outpost Substitute
    Uses the M01 Outpost plus set_building_capturable and make_indestructible.
    PASS confirms a replacement can preserve the Crown control-point mechanic.
